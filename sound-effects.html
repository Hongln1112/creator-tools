What is sound synthesis?

This tool generates audio with the Web Audio API, turning waveforms, frequencies and envelopes into sound — no samples or files needed. Think of it like mixing a drink: you pick the base ingredients and adjust the amounts until it tastes right.

How to use the synthesizer

Start from a preset sound, then shape it — change the waveform, pitch, length and volume. Use the recipe cards to combine several voices, and arrange them on the sequence timeline to build a short multi-step sound or effect loop. Play to preview, and adjust until it matches your scene.

Exporting

Export WAV downloads your sound as a file you can drop into a video or game. Because everything is generated on the fly, the same recipe can be tuned and re-exported endlessly.

When to use it

Use it for UI notification sounds, short game effects, app pings, video accents and any place you need a clean synthesized blip without hunting through a sample library.
