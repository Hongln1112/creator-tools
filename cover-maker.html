What is a cover image?

A cover is the first visual people see before they open your content — on a video, a blog post, a social profile or an event page. A strong cover states the topic and sets the mood in one glance.

How to use the cover generator

Choose an output size that matches your platform (video thumbs, social posts, banners), then build a background from a gradient or pattern. Add a short title, pick text size and placement, and adjust spacing until it reads clearly even at small sizes. Keep the key words large and central.

Exporting

Export PNG at the platform ratio, or copy the background CSS if you want to reuse the look inside a page. Everything you set is remembered in your browser, so you can return and fine-tune later.

When to use it

Use it for video thumbnails, article headers, playlist art, event and webinar banners, and social media headers — anywhere the first frame decides whether someone clicks.
