What this tool makes

This generator turns a word or short name into a finished logo or badge — text plus an optional shape, outline and effect. It is meant for quick branding needs: a site wordmark, a channel avatar, a tag sticker or a button icon, without opening a vector editor.

How to use it

Type your text, then choose a font weight and size. Optionally pick a shape — rectangle, rounded square, ellipse or a wavy edge — and adjust its corner radius and outline. Effects such as 3D, emboss and neon can be stacked to give the word depth or a glow. The preview updates as you go.

Exporting your design

  • Copy SVG — grab the vector markup to embed directly or keep as a scalable asset.
  • Export PNG — download a crisp image for avatars, favicons and social posts.

When to use it

Use it for product names, channel and shop logos, event tags, packaging labels and any place you need a readable wordmark fast.
