Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

Creator Tools is a set of static pages. Every generator runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect your designs, the text you type, your uploads, or any personal information through the tools themselves.

Some tools remember your last settings using your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
